About

My Journey

I started my career as a backend engineer, building APIs and services with NestJS. That's my day-to-day craft — clean architecture, solid data models, and reliable services.

Alongside that, I taught myself Flutter to build and publish my own mobile apps — from idea, to design, to the Play Store, to real users and real analytics. I also built a full web application using Google Apps Script, proving I can ship a working product on almost any stack when the problem calls for it.

I care about building things end-to-end: the API, the app, the store listing, and the person using it on the other end.
